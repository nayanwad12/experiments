# Prompt library: things students can say

Students can talk naturally. These show the level of detail that gets great results first time.

## Starting
- "Make a 30-second Instagram reel from `raw/take1.mp4`. Cut the pauses and ums, big pop captions, upbeat music, end card with @myhandle."
- "Launch video for my course *Vibe Editing*, 9:16, 25 s, bold and punchy, lime and black, ending with ENROLL NOW."
- "Turn these 6 AI clips in `raw/` into a 60 s brand film with my voiceover `raw/vo.mp3`. It shouldn't look like AI."
- "Personalised welcome videos for every name in `students.csv`."

## Directing the vibe
- "Make it feel **warm, punchy and a little dreamy**."
- "Pace it like a MrBeast intro for the first 3 seconds, then calm down."
- "More premium: slower moves, more space, serif titles, no bouncy stuff."
- "Make it look handmade, like crayon on paper."

## Precise feedback (fastest revisions)
- "At 0:04.2 the title lands late. Put it on the beat."
- "Captions: one size smaller, move them up a bit, highlight in orange instead of lime."
- "Cut the bit from 12.3 s to 15.0 s, it's a retake."
- "Swap the music for something calmer, keep the SFX."
- "Hold the end card 1 second longer."
- "Shot 3 has a weird hand. Cut around it or replace it with a graphic."

## Finishing
- "Export for Reels, YouTube and LinkedIn feed."
- "Give me a contact sheet of the final."
- "Make 3 hook variations for A/B testing."
- "Save this style as a template so the next one is a one-liner."

# VOX COLLAGE: art direction for "Vibe Editing, explained"

These notes come from 48 Pinterest pins, collected with `tools/pinterest-refs`. The pins themselves are gitignored.
I only studied them. Every prop in the film is drawn in code, and no image comes from a pin.

| Folder | Search | What I took from it |
|---|---|---|
| `vox/` | vox explainer video collage style frames | Grid-paper desk. Black-and-white halftone cutouts with white die-cut borders. One yellow highlighter swipe behind the key word. A typewriter label for the year or caption. A hand-drawn arrow or circle. |
| `collage/` | torn paper collage poster halftone editorial | Torn edges with the white paper core showing. Ransom-note lettering cut from different scraps. Tape holding things down. A red rubber stamp. Newsprint columns. |
| `papercut/` | paper cut out collage animation stop motion | Layered paper with real soft shadows. Movement on twos (12 fps) with a little jitter between exposures, so it looks hand-placed. |
| `riso/` | risograph paper texture illustration bold shapes | **Mostly dropped.** The riso palette (pink, blue, orange) fights the Vox look. I kept only the paper grain. |

## The rules

1. **Palette:** desk paper `#EEEAE1` with a blue-grey grid, cut-paper white `#FAF8F2`, ink `#161514`, newsprint
   `#D8D3C8`, dark slate `#2A2927`, **one** loud yellow `#FFD21F`, and marker red `#E0352B`. Red is used only for
   handwriting and stamps.
2. **Photos become print:** every object (film strip, scissors, clock, clip, clapperboard) is a greyscale drawing
   turned into newspaper halftone dots, then cut out with a wobbly white border and a soft shadow.
3. **Three hands write on the page:** typewriter tags (JetBrains Mono), big black sans headlines (Inter Tight 900)
   with a yellow swipe behind them, and red marker notes (Caveat Brush).
4. **Stop-motion:** paper moves on twos with a small jitter. The camera drift, shake and torn-paper wipes run at 30 fps.
5. **Every frame shares the same frame:** a chapter tag taped top-left (`CH.01 THE CUT` … `CH.05 THE SHIFT`),
   "VIBE EDITING, EXPLAINED" top-right, and torn-paper captions with the spoken word highlighted in yellow.
6. **Scene changes** are a torn sheet of newsprint or yellow paper sweeping up the screen.

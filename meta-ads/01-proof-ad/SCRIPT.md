# Meta Ad 01: "This video edited itself" (proof + workflow)

**Format:** 9:16 talking head, ~45–50 s. Also cut to 4:5 for Feed.
**Idea:** the ad *is* the proof. A split screen shows the RAW take next to the EDITED version, and then you walk the
viewer through the 4 steps of the workflow that made the video they're watching.
**CTA:** comment "VIBE" → they get a DM with the course link (or tap the button: see the CTA options at the end).

---

## The script

Lines in **bold** are what you say. *[Brackets]* are what I'll add in the edit. You don't need to do anything for those.

### 1 · HOOK (0–5 s)
**"This video you're watching right now… I didn't edit it. My system did."**

*[Split screen: left = your raw phone clip labelled RAW, right = the finished edit labelled EDITED BY VIBE EDITING.
Both play in sync.]*

### 2 · PROOF (5–11 s)
**"On the left is my raw recording. Pauses, ums, mistakes. On the right is what you're seeing. Same clip. I never
opened a timeline."**

*[On "pauses, ums, mistakes", the left side flashes red "CUT" stamps over the bits that were removed.]*

### 3 · SET UP THE STEPS (11–13 s)
**"Here's exactly how it works. Four steps."**

*[A "1 2 3 4" progress bar appears at the top and stays there.]*

### 4 · STEP 1: RECORD (13–19 s)
**"Step one. I record on my phone. One take. Mistakes are fine. Like this one, I'm going to… wait, let me say that
again. Mistakes are fine."**

*[You fumble on purpose. In the edit I show that fumble on the RAW side with a CUT stamp, while the EDITED side jumps
straight to the clean line. That's the proof, live.]*

### 5 · STEP 2: DESCRIBE THE VIBE (19–26 s)
**"Step two. I drop the clip in and tell it what I want in plain English: punchy reel, big captions, upbeat music."**

*[A prompt box types out: "punchy reel · big captions · upbeat music". You're shrunk into a corner bubble.]*

### 6 · STEP 3: IT EDITS (26–35 s)
**"Step three. The system does the editing. It cuts the silences, adds these zooms, these captions, the music…
even this graphic."**

*[Each thing appears on the exact word you say it: a punch-in on "zooms", the captions flash bigger on "captions",
the music swells on "music", and a sticker pops in on "graphic".]*

### 7 · STEP 4: REVIEW + EXPORT (35–42 s)
**"Step four. I watch the preview, say 'more energy' or 'shorter', and it exports for Reels, Feed and YouTube in one
go."**

*[The frame splits into 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9 versions of this same video.]*

### 8 · PAYOFF (42–45 s)
**"That's how this entire video was made."**

*[Back to the split screen one last time, RAW next to EDITED.]*

### 9 · CTA (45–50 s)
**"Want the exact system? Comment 'VIBE' and I'll send you the link."**

*[A big "Comment VIBE" card with a comment-box animation. Vibe Editing logo + Ideabro Studio.]*

## CTA options (record all three, it takes 30 seconds)
- **A (comment):** "Want the exact system? Comment 'VIBE' and I'll send you the link."
- **B (button):** "Want the exact system? Tap the button below and start today."
- **C (both):** "Want the exact system? Tap below, or comment 'VIBE' and I'll DM you the link."

Note: on paid ads the button (Learn More / Sign Up) is what Meta optimises for. Comment-to-DM works on Instagram ads,
but only if a DM automation (e.g. ManyChat) is set up on the account. B or C is the safer default.

## Extra hooks for A/B testing (record each, 1 take is enough)
1. "This video you're watching right now… I didn't edit it. My system did." *(main)*
2. "I recorded this on my phone. I didn't edit a single second of it."
3. "Left is what I recorded. Right is what you're watching. Here's what happened in between."

## How to record

**Setup**
- Phone **vertical**, 1080p or 4K, **30 fps**. Back camera if you can (better quality), at eye level.
- Face a window or a soft light. No strong light behind you.
- A quiet room. A lapel / wireless mic if you have one, phone mic is OK.
- Leave a little headroom above you and keep your face in the upper-middle of the frame (captions go lower).

**While recording**
- Record everything in **one long file**, section by section. Don't stop the camera between lines.
- Pause about 1 second between sections. Say a line 2–3 times if it didn't feel right, and I'll keep the best one.
- **Keep the deliberate fumble in step 1.** It's part of the proof.
- Energy a bit higher than normal conversation. Smile on the hook.
- Point at the screen on "these zooms", "these captions", "this graphic" (makes it feel like it's happening live).

**Also send (optional, makes the ad stronger)**
- A 10 s screen recording of you typing a prompt into Claude Code. If you skip this, I'll recreate the screen in
  graphics.
- Your logo (PNG) and brand colours, if they differ from the Vibe Editing house style.

**Upload:** send the raw files as they are (no compression, no edits). I'll keep the untouched raw take for the RAW
side of the split screen, so the comparison is genuine.

# Ad D · "I'm going to ruin this video on purpose" (about 38 seconds)

**What this ad does:** you record the worst take you can: umms, coughs, restarts, a phone ringing, lost lines. The
viewer watches the mess happen. Then the video rewinds and plays the **same take** back clean, with captions, zooms
and music. The clean part also explains how you work, and the ad ends with an invitation to comment. There's no price
and no "buy" in the ad.

**Why it works:** the viewer has seen the raw mess with their own eyes, so the clean version can't be faked. That's
the proof. They see *that* the system fixed it, but not *how*. That's the curiosity gap.

---

## Part 1 · Recording order (what you actually say, in one continuous take)

Phone vertical, on a stand, and don't move it. Record everything below **in one go**, top to bottom.

> **The golden rule:** every mistake is followed by a **1-second pause**, then the **full clean sentence**. Never fix
> half a sentence. The cough, the phone ring and the door slam must happen **between** sentences, never over one.
> That way the system can lift out every clean line perfectly.

| # | What you do / say | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"I'm going to record the worst video of my life… and my system has to fix it."** | The hook. Clean, confident, small smile. Pause 1 s. |
| 2 | **"Let's go."** | Rub your hands together or crack your knuckles. Pause 1 s. |
| 3 | *Mistake:* "So, umm… the way I make… umm… my videos is… uh…" *(stop)* "Wait. Let me start again." *(pause 1 s)* **"I record one take on my phone."** | Real, natural umms. Don't overact. |
| 4 | *Mistake:* turn away and cough twice. *(pause 1 s)* **"No editing app. No editor."** | |
| 5 | *Mistake:* "And then I… what was it…" *(look off camera, 2 s of silence, look back)* **"I tell my system the vibe, in plain words."** | Losing your line is the most relatable mistake. Let the silence feel awkward. |
| 6 | *Mistake:* a second phone or an alarm rings beside you. "Sorry, sorry…" *(silence it, pause 1 s)* **"Make it punchy. Big captions. Upbeat music."** | Set a timer on another phone before you start. Say this line like you're giving an order. |
| 7 | *Mistake:* "And it does all the… and it does the…" *(pause 1 s)* **"And it does everything else. The cuts, the captions, the music."** | Two false starts, then the clean line. |
| 8 | *Mistake:* a door slams, or someone calls your name off camera. Flinch, laugh. *(pause 2 s)* | Ask someone at home to do this on a signal. No line here, just the reaction. |
| 9 | *(look straight into the camera, deadpan, then exhale)* **"Okay. That was a disaster."** *(beat)* **"Watch what my system does with it."** | The turning point. Slower, serious, one eyebrow up. Pause 2 s. |
| 10 | **"Same take. I didn't cut a thing. My system did."** | Calm and proud. Pause 1 s. |
| 11 | **"I've started letting a few creators use it."** | Pause 1 s. |
| 12 | **"Want to see how it works? Comment SYSTEM and I'll send it to you."** | The comment CTA. Smile. Pause 2 s. |
| 13 | **"Tap below and I'll show you how my system works."** | The button CTA, for the second version of the ad. |

Then do the whole thing **one more time** as a backup take. Mistakes can change; the clean lines stay word for word.

## Part 2 · The finished ad (what viewers see)

### Scene 1 · The hook (0 – 3 sec)
**They hear:** line 1, "I'm going to record the worst video of my life… and my system has to fix it."
**They see:** the raw take with a red ● REC dot and a running timer. Big text: **WORST TAKE EVER vs MY SYSTEM**.
A small counter appears in the corner: **MISTAKES: 0**.

### Scene 2 · The chaos (3 – 13 sec)
**They hear:** lines 2 to 8, raw, every umm, cough and ring left in.
**They see:** the mess in fast-forward (about 2× with a ⏩ icon), dropping to normal speed for the funniest moments
("Wait. Let me start again", the phone ringing, the flinch at the door). Each mistake gets a sticker as it happens:
**UMM ×4 · RESTART · COUGH · LOST LINE · PHONE · RESTART ×2 · DOOR**, and the counter ticks up with each one.

### Scene 3 · The turn (13 – 17 sec)
**They hear:** line 9, "Okay. That was a disaster… Watch what my system does with it."
**They see:** normal speed, a slow push-in on your face. The counter freezes on the final number.

### Scene 4 · The rewind (17 – 18 sec)
**They see:** a VHS-style rewind of the whole chaos, backwards and fast, with tape-rewind sound.

### Scene 5 · The same take, clean (18 – 30 sec)
**They hear:** only the clean lines, back to back, with no pauses:
"I record one take on my phone. No editing app. No editor. I tell my system the vibe, in plain words. Make it punchy.
Big captions. Upbeat music. And it does everything else. The cuts, the captions, the music."
**They see:**
- A small **RAW** window in the corner keeps playing the messy original, so the viewer knows it's the same take.
- It starts simple: small captions, no music.
- On **"Make it punchy. Big captions. Upbeat music."** a prompt box types that line out, then the edit obeys: captions
  become big pop captions, a punch-in zoom lands, and the music drops in on the beat.
- On "the cuts, the captions, the music", each word flashes as its own pop-up.

### Scene 6 · The payoff (30 – 33 sec)
**They hear:** line 10, "Same take. I didn't cut a thing. My system did."
**They see:** a quick split: **RAW** on the left (the chaos) and **MY SYSTEM** on the right (clean).

### Scene 7 · CTA (33 – 38 sec)
**They hear:** lines 11 and 12 (or 13 for the button version).
**They see:** the receipt end card, with real numbers from this edit:
`Mistakes removed: 9 · Umms cut: 6 · Restarts: 3 · Silences cut: 22 · Timeline opened: 0`
then **THE VIBE EDITING SYSTEM** and **Comment "SYSTEM"** (or **Tap below ↓**).

*(The numbers above are placeholders. The real ones come from the edit of your take.)*

## Recording tips for this one
- **Make the mistakes believable.** Real umms and real awkward silences beat cartoon ones. If something really goes
  wrong, keep going; that's even better.
- **Keep the clean lines word for word.** They're the script viewers actually hear in Scene 5.
- **Don't move the phone.** A fixed frame makes the clean version look seamless.
- **Sound:** a lapel mic if you have one. Let the phone ring and the door slam be loud; they're part of the joke.
- **Send the raw file untouched.** The raw take is the proof, so it shows up on screen as-is.

## Rules check
- Says "my system" every time, never "course", "app" or "tool".
- No price, no "buy", no "enroll", no tool names, no income claims.
- Every number on screen is real, taken from this edit.

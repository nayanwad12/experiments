# Ad D · "My editing setup" (the pool ad, ~45 s)

**Concept:** everyone else's editing setup is a desk, two screens and a timeline. Yours is a swimming pool. While you
swim, your system edits. When you get out, the video is done. The ad sells **freedom**, and the twist at the end is that
this video was edited by the same system.

**Make it true:** before you get in the pool, really send a video to your system (record a short clip, give it one
line). When you get out, it's finished, and we film you checking it. Everything the ad says actually happened.

## Shot list (what to film)
1. **Hook:** tight close-up of your face (looks like a normal "talking to camera" shot), then a wide shot showing the
   pool. Film both from the same spot so the cut feels like a reveal.
2. **Talking at the pool edge:** lines 2 and 4. Phone on a tripod 1–1.5 m away, at water level.
3. **Swimming lap:** 8–10 s, filmed from the side or from above, no talking.
4. **Floating / on the steps:** line 5.
5. **Getting out:** climb out, towel, pick up the phone, smile at the screen.
6. **Phone screen close-up:** the finished video playing on your phone (record the screen too, as a backup).
7. **Pool chair:** sunglasses on, lines 7 and 8.
8. **Extras (B-roll):** splash, water surface, feet in the water, a drink on the pool chair. 3–5 s each.

## Recording tips for the pool
- **Sound is the hardest part.** Speak your lines out of the water or at the edge, close to the phone, when nobody else
  is splashing. A wireless clip-on mic helps (keep it dry). I'll clean the background noise in the edit.
- Backup plan: if the sound is bad, record the same lines again indoors as a voiceover and I'll lay it over the pool
  shots.
- Phone in a waterproof pouch or on a tripod well away from splashes. Vertical, 30 fps, 4K if possible.
- Shoot in morning or late-afternoon light. Midday sun makes harsh shadows on your face.
- Memorise one line at a time and film in short pieces. You don't need one long take for this ad.
- Pool rules: make sure filming is allowed and that no other people are recognisable in the background.

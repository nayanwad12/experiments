# Ad E · "Just say it" / "Bas bolo" (about 35 seconds)

**One simple idea:** you don't need to know editing. You record, you say what you want, and the system edits.
The ad is edited by the system, so the ad itself is the proof.

**Flow:** Hook → Problem → My system → 3 steps → Why it's easy → Proof → Comment SYSTEM

**Record:** phone vertical, one take, 1-second pause after every line. Same script in two languages, pick one
(or record both and test them against each other).

## How to say it
- **Line 1:** look straight into the camera, small smile. This line decides if people keep watching.
- **Line 2:** a little tired and annoyed. Everyone who edits knows this pain.
- **Lines 4–6:** count on your fingers (1, 2, 3). Clear and slow.
- **Line 7:** the most important line. Slow down and say it with confidence.
- **Line 9:** friendly, like telling a friend a secret.

## Rules check
- Always "my system". Never "course", "app", "tool" or tool names.
- No price, no "buy", no "enroll", no income claims. The selling happens after they comment.
- Line 8 must be true. If not every video on your page was made this way, say "This video was made exactly like that."

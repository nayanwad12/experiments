# Ad C · "Before / After" split-screen (about 50 seconds)

**What this ad does:** the viewer sees two versions of the same video side by side: the video straight from your
phone (BEFORE) and the edited video (AFTER). While they watch, you explain the 4 simple steps your system uses. At the
end you invite them to comment to learn more. There's no price and no "buy" in the ad.

**The screen during the whole ad:**
- **Left side:** "BEFORE: straight from my phone" (your real, unedited recording)
- **Right side:** "AFTER: edited by my system" (the finished version)

---

## Scene by scene

### Scene 1 · The hook (0 – 5 sec)
**You say:** "This video you're watching right now… I didn't edit it. My system did."
**Viewers see:** the screen splits into BEFORE and AFTER.
**How to say it:** look straight into the camera. Calm, confident, small smile.

### Scene 2 · Show the difference (5 – 11 sec)
**You say:** "On the left is the video straight from my phone. On the right is what you're watching. Same video. No
editing app."
**Viewers see:** red "CUT" marks appear on the left side wherever you paused or made a mistake.
**How to say it:** point left when you say "left" and right when you say "right".

### Scene 3 · Introduce the steps (11 – 14 sec)
**You say:** "I built this system for my own videos. It works in four simple steps."
**Viewers see:** the numbers 1 2 3 4 appear at the top of the screen.

### Scene 4 · Step 1: Record (14 – 22 sec)
**You say:** "Step one. I record on my phone. Just one take. Mistakes are okay. Like right now, I'm going to…
sorry, let me say that again. Mistakes are okay."
**Viewers see:** on the left, your mistake gets a red CUT stamp. On the right, the video skips it smoothly.
**How to say it:** make the mistake on purpose but make it look natural. Then say the line again, cleanly.

### Scene 5 · Step 2: Tell it what you want (22 – 28 sec)
**You say:** "Step two. I tell my system what I want, in simple words. Like: make it short, add big text, add music."
**Viewers see:** a typing box writes out "make it short, add big text, add music".

### Scene 6 · Step 3: It edits (28 – 36 sec)
**You say:** "Step three. The system edits it for me. It cuts the pauses, zooms in, adds the text, the music… even
this."
**Viewers see:** each thing happens exactly when you say it: a zoom on "zooms in", the text grows on "text", the music
gets louder on "music", and a sticker pops up on "even this".
**How to say it:** on "even this", point to the empty space beside your head. That's where the sticker appears.

### Scene 7 · Step 4: Check and done (36 – 42 sec)
**You say:** "Step four. I check it. Want a change? I just say it. Then it's ready for Instagram, YouTube, anywhere."
**Viewers see:** the video appears in different sizes (tall, square, wide), ready for every app.

### Scene 8 · The payoff (42 – 46 sec)
**You say:** "That's how this whole video was made. And every video on my page."
**Viewers see:** back to the BEFORE / AFTER split one last time.

### Scene 9 · Call to action (46 – 52 sec)
**You say:** "Want to see how my system works? Comment SYSTEM and I'll send you the details."
**Viewers see:** the end card:
- real numbers from this edit, e.g. *Pauses cut: 41 · Mistakes removed: 3 · Editing apps opened: 0*
- **THE VIBE EDITING SYSTEM**
- **Comment "SYSTEM"**

**How to say it:** slow down a little, smile, nod at the end.

**Record this second ending too (for the button version of the ad):**
"Want to see how my system works? Tap the button below."

## Before you hit record
- [ ] Phone standing up (vertical), at eye level, 30 fps
- [ ] Face a window or a soft light. Nothing bright behind you.
- [ ] Quiet room. Use a clip-on mic if you have one.
- [ ] Record the whole script in **one go** without stopping the camera. Pause 1 second between scenes.
- [ ] Didn't like a line? Just say it again. I'll keep the best one.
- [ ] Keep the planned mistake in Step 1. It's part of the proof.
- [ ] Record both endings (comment + button).
- [ ] Send the original file, not compressed (Google Drive or WeTransfer, not WhatsApp).

# Meta Ads: recording scripts (soft sell for the Vibe Editing System)

## Positioning (applies to every ad)
We're selling a **proprietary editing system**, but the ads never pitch it directly. Each ad:
1. **Proves it:** the ad itself was edited by the system, and the viewer can see that.
2. **Owns it:** it's always "**my** system" / "the system I built". It's never a course, a tool or an app.
3. **Shows the what, not the how:** the steps are visible, but the inside of the system stays a curiosity gap.
4. **Soft CTA:** "comment SYSTEM" or "tap below to see how it works". No price, no "buy", no "enroll" in the ad.
   The selling happens on the next step (DM, landing page or webcast).

**Words to avoid in the ads:** course, buy, price, discount, enroll, any tool names (Claude, Premiere, CapCut).
**Don't claim** anything that isn't true for you (hours saved, "last year", income). The ⚠️ lines are placeholders to
replace with your real story. Meta also rejects income or "get rich" claims.

**End card on all three ("the receipt"):** real numbers from my edit of your take, e.g.
`Silences cut: 41 · Mistakes removed: 3 · Captions: 96 words · Formats: 4 · Timeline opened: 0`
then **THE VIBE EDITING SYSTEM** plus the soft CTA.

## Ad A · "Say it and it happens" (~45 s)
*The wow ad. Every instruction you speak happens on screen.*

| # | You say | On screen (my edit) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Everything I say in this video is going to happen. Watch."** | Plain raw look: no captions, no music |
| 2 | **"Add captions."** | Captions snap on |
| 3 | **"Zoom in."** | Punch-in |
| 4 | **"Drop the beat."** | Music drops in |
| 5 | **"I'm not editing this. Nobody is. This is the editing system I built for my own videos."** | Title: MY EDITING SYSTEM |
| 6 | **"Make it black and white."** *(pause, look at camera)* **"Okay, too much. Bring the colour back."** | B&W, then colour returns |
| 7 | **"Here's what's actually happening. I record one raw take on my phone. I tell my system the vibe in plain words. And it does everything else: the cuts, the captions, the music, the effects. I just direct."** | Steps 1-2-3 as icons; RAW clip flashes on "raw take" |
| 8 | **"Now put me somewhere else."** | Background swaps behind you |
| 9 | **"Every video on my page is made exactly like this."** | Quick wall of your other videos |
| 10 | **"I've started opening this system up to a few creators. Want to see how it works? Comment SYSTEM and I'll send it to you."** | Receipt end card + "Comment SYSTEM" |

**Recording note:** after each command (lines 2, 3, 4, 6, 8), hold still for 1 second so the effect has room to land.

## Ad B · "Reply to a comment" (~40 s)
*The native ad. It looks like a reply Reel, not an ad.*

*[Opens with an Instagram comment sticker: "bro who edits your videos?? 🔥"]*

1. **"Honestly? Nobody. Not me, not an editor. My system did."**
2. ⚠️ **"I built my own editing system because editing was eating my whole week."** *(swap in your real reason)*
3. **"Here's how this exact video got made. I recorded it on my phone, one take, mistakes and all."**
   *[The raw take flashes on screen with your mistakes visible]*
4. **"I gave it to my system with one line: make it punchy, big captions, upbeat music."**
   *[That line types out in a prompt box]*
5. **"And it did all of this. Cut the pauses, removed my mistakes, added the zooms, the captions, the music. Everything
   you're seeing."** *[Each element pops on its word]*
6. **"I don't open an editing app anymore. I just watch it happen."**
7. **"A lot of you have been asking about it, so I'm letting a few people use it. Comment SYSTEM and I'll DM you how it
   works."** *[Receipt end card]*

## CTA alternatives (record each once, at the end of the session)
- **Comment:** "Comment SYSTEM and I'll send you how it works." *(needs DM automation such as ManyChat on Instagram ads)*
- **Button:** "Tap the button below to see how my system works."
- **Both:** "Tap below, or comment SYSTEM, and I'll show you how it works."

Paid ads optimise best on the button. The comment version builds social proof in the comments. Test both.

## Funnel this assumes
Ad (proof + curiosity) → DM or landing page "See how the system works" → where the selling happens (demo video or
webcast, then the offer with the price). The ads only have to earn the click.

## How to record
- Phone vertical, 1080p or 4K, 30 fps, eye level, facing a window. A lapel mic if you have one.
- One continuous file per ad. Pause 1 s between lines. Repeat any line that felt off (I keep the best one).
- Energy a little above normal conversation. Smile on the hook.
- Send the files raw and uncompressed. The untouched take becomes the RAW footage in the proof shots.
- Optional: 10 s of yourself typing a prompt on your laptop (screen recording). If you skip it, I'll recreate it.

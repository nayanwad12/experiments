# The 30-Day Vibe Editing Challenge
One small video a day. By day 30 you have a portfolio, a showreel and real confidence.
Post each day in the community with **#VibeDay[number]**.

## Week 1: Foundations
| day | challenge | skill |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Install everything and render the branded test frames | Start Here |
| 2 | 15-second clip with pauses and ums cut | Talking-head |
| 3 | Same clip with pop captions in your colours | Talking-head |
| 4 | Add an upbeat music bed under your voice | Audio |
| 5 | Write a brief + beat table for a 20-second promo | Brief |
| 6 | Render the promo from day 5 | Launch |
| 7 | Rest day: post your week-1 before/after | — |

## Rules
- Done is better than perfect. 15 seconds counts.
- Always post the prompt you used (it helps everyone).
- Comment on 2 other people's posts every day.

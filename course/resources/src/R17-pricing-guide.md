# Pricing & Packages Guide
Price the **outcome and the speed**, not your hours. The numbers below are *example starting points*. Adjust them to your market, niche and portfolio.

## Package ideas
| package | what's included | example starting point |
|---|---|---|
| Starter Reel | 1 talking-head reel, captions, music, 1 revision | ₹1,500 – ₹3,000 |
| Reel Pack (monthly) | 8–12 reels/month, captions, hooks, music | ₹15,000 – ₹40,000 / month |
| Launch Kit | 25 s promo + 2 hooks + 3 formats + cut-downs | ₹15,000 – ₹50,000 |
| Motion Pack | logo reveal + 5 lower-thirds + transitions (transparent) | ₹8,000 – ₹25,000 |
| Brand Film (AI footage) | 45–90 s film, VO, graphics, grade, sound | ₹25,000 – ₹1,00,000 |
| Personalised Campaign | template + per-video rendering | setup fee + ₹50–₹300 per video |
| Podcast Clips | 5–10 clips per episode | ₹5,000 – ₹15,000 / episode |

## Rules that protect you
- 50% upfront. Always.
- Revisions = 2 rounds included; extra rounds are paid.
- Retainers beat one-offs: offer a monthly pack after every successful project.
- Never compete on being cheapest. Compete on speed, quality and reliability.

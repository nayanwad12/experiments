# The Vibe Loop: One-Pager
Follow it on every project. It's how you get it right on the first render.

| # | step | you say | you check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Brief** | what, who, where, vibe, brand | Claude's questions answered |
| 2 | **Beat table** | "draft a beat table, don't render yet" | every second planned, 1 idea per row |
| 3 | **Build** | "build it" | — |
| 4 | **Stills** | "show me stills of each section" | layout, typos, safe zones, colours |
| 5 | **Draft** | "render a draft" | timing, pace, sound sync |
| 6 | **Final** | "render the final" | contact sheet of the final file |
| 7 | **Export** | "export for Reels, Shorts and YouTube" | sizes, loudness, file names |

## Golden rules
- Never approve what you haven't seen as stills.
- One change = one sentence with a timestamp.
- Keep every version (`_v1`, `_v2`…). Never overwrite what a client has seen.
- All timings live in one file (`timeline.py`), so picture and sound never drift.
- Taste is your job. Keyframes are the AI's job.

# Motion & Easing Cheat Sheet

## Pick the easing by intent
| intent | easing | duration |
|---|---|---|
| arrive (UI, text) | ease-out cubic / expo | 0.3–0.5 s |
| playful arrive | ease-out back / spring | 0.4–0.6 s |
| leave | ease-in cubic | 0.2–0.35 s |
| move across | ease-in-out | 0.5–0.9 s |
| land / drop | bounce | 0.6–0.9 s |
| constant drift | linear (only here!) | — |

## Timing rules
- Exits are faster than entrances.
- Stagger groups by 60–120 ms.
- Anticipation: a small move back before a big move forward.
- Hold important frames ≥ 1 s (reading time = 0.3 s/word + 0.5 s).
- Entrances on beats, big changes on bars.
- One focal point moving at a time.

## Kinetic type moves
word slam · stagger rise · highlight bar · typewriter + caret · counter · strike-through · slot machine · masked reveal

## Transitions
hard cut on beat · whip pan · iris/circle wipe · shape wipe · zoom-through · glitch · match cut

## Camera
slow push-in (+3–6% over a shot) · handheld drift (tiny noise) · impact shake (decays in 0.4 s)

## Common mistakes
everything the same speed · linear motion · too many fonts/colours · text touching the frame edge · no hold time

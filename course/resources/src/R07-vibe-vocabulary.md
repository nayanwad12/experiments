# Vibe Vocabulary: Feelings → Instructions
Say the feeling. This is what the AI does with it.

| you say | cuts & pace | motion | type | colour & look | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **punchy** | hard cuts on beats, 1–2 s shots | slams, 0.2–0.4 s moves, shakes | heavy condensed (Anton) | high contrast | impacts, hype bed |
| **premium** | longer shots, calm | slow ease-in-out, lots of space | serif + thin sans | muted, soft grade | cinematic/corporate, quiet |
| **playful** | bouncy, quick | overshoot, squash & stretch | rounded, hand fonts | bright, saturated | glockenspiel, pops, boings |
| **cinematic** | slow, deliberate | push-ins, drifts | serif titles | letterbox, teal-orange, grain | drones, risers, hits |
| **dreamy** | dissolves | slow floats, blur | light serif/italic | pastel, glow, soft focus | chill bed, reverb |
| **techy** | snappy | glitches, grids, scans | mono | dark + neon accent | tech bed, clicks, glitches |
| **clean** | medium | simple ease-out | one sans family | white space, brand colours | light corporate |
| **raw / authentic** | natural pauses kept | handheld feel | simple captions | natural, light grade | room tone, soft bed |
| **luxury** | very slow | elegant reveals | wide-tracked serif caps | black/gold/cream | strings-like pads |
| **energetic** | fast montage | whip pans, zooms | big bold caps | bold primaries | hype, risers, drops |
| **cozy** | gentle | soft bounces | rounded | warm grade | chill/lo-fi |
| **handmade** | on twos (12 fps) | boil, wobble | hand-lettered | paper/clay texture | foley, ukulele-like plucks |

## Combining
- Use 2–3 words max: *"premium, calm, warm"*.
- Avoid contradictions: *"calm but frantic"*.
- Add a reference for precision: *"punchy like a Nike ad, clean like Apple."*

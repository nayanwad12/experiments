# Caption Style Guide
Most social video is watched with the sound off. Captions are part of the design.

## The 4 styles
| style | looks like | use for | default size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **pop** | 1–3 BIG words, active word highlighted | Reels, TikTok, Shorts | ~12% of width |
| **karaoke** | a line fills with colour as spoken | quotes, lyrics, hooks | ~8% |
| **clean** | sentence subtitles on a soft box | YouTube, interviews, courses | ~5% |
| **minimal** | small outlined subtitles | brand films, cinematic | ~4% |

## Rules
- Never cover eyes or mouth. Position around 65–70% down the frame on vertical video.
- Keep clear of the platform UI: **top 250 px, bottom 420 px, right 140 px** (9:16).
- Highlight **emphasis words** (numbers, promises, emotions) in the accent colour, 1–3 per sentence.
- Pop captions: UPPERCASE, no trailing commas or full stops.
- Clean subtitles: sentence case, max 2 lines, max ~42 characters per line.
- Spell names right (fix the transcript first).
- Always also export the `.srt` file for YouTube/LinkedIn (accessibility + search).

## Prompts
- "Pop captions, brand colours, highlight free, secret, 10x."
- "Captions one size smaller and 10% higher."
- "Clean subtitles for the YouTube version and give me the .srt."

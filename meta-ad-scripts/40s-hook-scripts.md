# Vibe Editing System — 40-Second Hook-First Ad Scripts (v3)

Structure for every script:

| Time | Beat | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **HOOK** | Call out their exact problem, with the strongest visual effect on screen at the same time |
| 3–10 s | **AGITATE** | Make the problem hurt (time, money, views) |
| 10–28 s | **SOLUTION** | Vibe editing: what it does, in plain words |
| 28–35 s | **PROOF** | "This video was edited by the system" |
| 35–40 s | **CTA** | One clear action |

About 100 spoken words each, about 40 s after the cut.
Each script has 2 alternative hooks. Record all three opening lines so Meta can A/B test them.

## Which one to run first
- **Script 3:** strongest visual hook (before/after inside 3 s), and it works best muted. Lead with this.
- **Script 1:** broadest pain (time). Use it for cold audiences.
- **Script 2:** sharpest buyer intent (money). Use it for business owners and retargeting.

## Comparison reel ending (swap in for the last 2 lines of any script)
```
Top is what I recorded. Bottom is what the system made.
Tap Learn More.
```

## Recording checklist
- [ ] Tripod, 4K 30 fps, exposure and focus locked, plain wall 1.5–2 m behind you
- [ ] Say the **first line with energy**, straight into the lens. It's 80% of the ad
- [ ] Record each alternative hook 2–3 times
- [ ] Script 3: hold still during the "Watch." pause (that's where the snap happens)
- [ ] Record the comparison ending separately, in the same spot

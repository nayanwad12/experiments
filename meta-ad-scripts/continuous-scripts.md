# Vibe Editing System — Continuous Talking-Head Scripts (v2)

One flowing talk to camera per script, explaining what vibe editing is, what's involved and how
someone can start. The edit happens around you while you talk. At the end you reveal that this video
was edited by the same system, and the same take becomes the comparison reel.

Line breaks = natural breaths. Speak at your normal pace.
Each script is about 50–60 s raw, about 45–55 s after the cut.

---

## SCRIPT A — "Editing just changed"
*Angle: the big shift, from editing to directing. Best for cold audiences.*

```
Video editing just changed.
And most people haven't noticed yet.

For years, editing meant one thing.
Sit in front of a timeline, drag clips, set keyframes…
and spend six hours on a sixty-second reel.

Vibe editing flips that.
You don't edit anymore. You direct.

You tell the system what you want — the same way you'd brief a top editor.
Cut the pauses. Make the captions punchy.
Put the title behind me. Give it a cinematic look.
Add music that builds.

And it builds it.
Every cut, every caption, every sound — made in code, right on your laptop.

And it's not just reels.
The same system makes motion graphics, product ads, claymation, VFX,
podcast clips… even fifty versions of one ad, so you can test what works.

So what do you actually need?
Your footage. Your laptop.
And the Vibe Editing System — the editing skills, plus a full course that shows you how to direct every one of them.

No After Effects. No Premiere. No keyframes.

And this video you just watched?
I didn't open a single editing app.
I recorded it on my phone… and the system did the rest.
```

**What the edit does while you talk**
- "just changed": hard cut to a punch-in, with the title slamming in behind you
- "timeline… keyframes… six hours": an old-school timeline fills the background, clutters up, then crumples away
- "You don't edit anymore. You direct.": a freeze, then clean type: EDIT → ~~EDIT~~ **DIRECT**
- The brief lines ("Cut the pauses…"): each instruction types into a prompt box and **happens right then** (pause cut, captions switch on, title goes behind you, grade turns cinematic, music drops in)
- "not just reels": a fast montage of tiles (motion graphics, ad, clay, VFX, podcast) with a 50-tile grid on "fifty versions"
- "No After Effects…": three app icons crossed out on the beat
- Ending: freeze, rewind effect, the **raw take** appears, then the end card

## SCRIPT B — "What happens inside a vibe edit"
*Angle: the how-it-works walk-through. Best for people who are already curious, and for retargeting.*

```
Here's what actually happens inside a vibe edit.
Step by step.

Step one — you drop in your raw footage.
One take. Mistakes and all.

Step two — the system watches and listens.
It transcribes every word, finds your strongest line,
and moves it to the front.
Because the first two seconds decide everything.

Step three — the story cut.
Pauses, "umm"s, retakes… gone. Down to the exact word.

Step four — the look.
Captions that hit on your voice.
Text behind you. Your background swapped.
Zooms on the lines that matter.

Step five — sound.
Original music, whooshes, hits — generated from scratch.
So no copyright claims. Ever.

And step six — the part most editors never give you — proof.
Before anything renders, it shows you stills of every key moment.
You say "more energy", or "cleaner"… and it changes.

Then one command exports it for Reels, Feed, YouTube — every format.

That's vibe editing.
You bring the taste. The system does the keyframes.

And yes — this exact video went through all six steps.
Want to see what I actually recorded?
```

**What the edit does while you talk**
- A **step tracker** (1–6) sits on the side and fills in as you say each step
- Step 1: your raw clip drops into a folder icon
- Step 2: a live transcript scrolls next to you, your best line gets highlighted and flies to the top
- Step 3: a timeline with red silence blocks, snipped out word by word
- Step 4: each feature happens as you name it (captions on, text behind you, background swap, punch-in)
- Step 5: a waveform with SFX pins, and the music swells
- Step 6: a contact sheet of 9 stills, a typed "more energy", then the frame gets punchier
- "every format": your frame snaps through 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9
- Ending: hard cut to the raw take, then the end card

*Angle: value and money. Best for creators, coaches, brands and freelance editors.*

```
What does a one-lakh-rupee editor actually give you?

It's not just cuts.
It's a hook that stops the scroll.
Captions timed to every single word.
Motion graphics. A grade that makes phone footage look like film.
Sound design you can feel.
And then — versions of it for every platform.

That's days of work.
That's why it costs that much.

Now here's the shift.
Every one of those things… can be described in a sentence.
And if it can be described — it can be directed.

That's vibe editing.
You describe the edit like a creative director,
and an AI editor builds it — frame by frame, in code.

So who is this for?
Creators who post every day and can't afford an editor.
Coaches and brands who need ads tested fast.
And editors — because the ones who learn to direct
will deliver in hours what used to take days.

To start, you need three things.
Your footage. Your laptop.
And the Vibe Editing System — the skills, and the course that teaches you how to direct them.

And this video?
Every cut, every caption, every effect, every sound —

```

**What the edit does while you talk**
- Opening: a gold **₹1,00,000** stamp lands behind you
- The list ("hook… captions… motion… grade… sound… versions"): each item builds a **price-tag stack** beside you, and each feature appears on you as you say it
- "days of work": a calendar flips through days
- "described in a sentence": the price stack collapses into one typed prompt line
- "who is this for": three quick cards (Creators / Coaches & Brands / Editors)
- "three things": 1-2-3 counter: footage, laptop, Vibe Editing System box
- "directed, not edited": a freeze, then a split wipe to the raw take, then the end card

## Endings: same take, three uses

Record the script once. Then use the ending that fits each version:

**1. Final edit (ad), add after the last line:**
```
The Vibe Editing System. Tap Learn More.
```

**2. Comparison reel, swap the last 2–3 lines for:**
```
On top is what I recorded.
At the bottom is what the system made.
Same take. Zero timeline.
Tap Learn More.
```

**3. Organic post (comment-to-DM), end with:**
```
Comment "VIBE" and I'll send you how it works.
```

Tip: record all three endings straight after the main take, in the same position and light, so I can swap them in cleanly.

---

## Recording checklist
- [ ] Phone on a tripod, 4K 30 fps, exposure and focus locked
- [ ] Plain wall 1.5–2 m behind you, with space above your head for text behind you
- [ ] Solid-colour clothes, soft light from the front, no filters or beauty mode
- [ ] Talk like you're explaining it to a friend. Small mistakes are fine, the system cuts them
- [ ] Look at the lens, not the screen
- [ ] 2–3 full takes per script, plus the three endings

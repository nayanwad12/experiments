# Vibe Editing System — Meta Ad Scripts (v1)

Three talking-head ads. Each one ships as two videos:
- **Final edit** (9:16, plus a 4:5 cut for the Feed)
- **Comparison reel** (raw take next to the final edit)

How each reel is laid out:
1. **Teleprompter**: only the words you say. Copy it into a teleprompter app.
2. **Director's script**: what you say, what appears on screen, and the sound, second by second.
3. **Comparison reel**: how the side-by-side version plays.
4. **Ad copy**: primary text, headline and CTA for Ads Manager.
5. **Recording checklist**

`[PAUSE]` means stop for about 1 second, which gives the effect room to land. `[ACTION]` is something you physically do.

### 1C. Comparison reel (about 40 s)
- **Layout:** stacked vertically. Top: **RAW** (your phone take as recorded). Bottom: **VIBE EDITED**.
- **0–2 s hook text across the middle:** "Left: what I filmed. Right: what I got." (on a stacked layout: "Top / Bottom")
- The raw half plays honestly: you pointing at nothing, holding an empty palm, walking out of an empty room. That contrast sells it.
- **A prompt strip** runs between the two halves and types the instruction for each effect the moment it fires:
  `put my name behind me` → `swap the background to a neon city at night` → `track the logo to my palm` → `speed ramp the coin toss` → `disintegrate me into particles`
- **Last 3 s:** a stats card: *6 effects · 0 keyframes · 1 take*.

### 1D. Ad copy
- **Primary text:** Everything I said in this video happened. Name behind me, new background, a logo tracking my hand, slow-mo, vanishing into particles. No After Effects, no keyframes. I just said what I wanted. That's Vibe Editing.
- **Headline:** You talk. It edits.
- **CTA button:** Learn More

### 1E. Recording checklist (Reel 1)
- [ ] Phone on a **tripod, completely locked**. No touching it between lines.
- [ ] 4K, 30 fps, exposure and focus locked (long-press to set AE/AF lock).
- [ ] Stand **1.5–2 m in front of a plain wall**. Leave space above your head for the name.
- [ ] Solid-colour T-shirt that contrasts with the wall. No stripes.
- [ ] Soft light from the front (window or ring light). No bright window behind you.
- [ ] **Clean plate:** record 3 s of the empty room before you step in, and stay out 3 s when you "disappear".
- [ ] Palm shot: open palm flat to the camera, fingers together, move slowly.
- [ ] Coin toss: toss it about 30 cm up, in front of your chest.
- [ ] Record **3 full takes**. Mistakes are fine, the edit cuts them.

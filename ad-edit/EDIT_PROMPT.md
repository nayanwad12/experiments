# Generic full-edit prompt

Paste this with any raw talking-head video. Attach a design system image if you have one.

```
Edit the attached raw video into a premium, finished Instagram Reel / Meta ad. Deliver exactly two files:
1. The final edited reel
2. A comparison reel (raw take next to the final edit, playing in sync)

DESIGN SYSTEM
- If a design system / reference image is attached: build the whole edit in that style. Study it first and
  pull out its colour palette, typefaces (or the closest free fonts), layout habits, shapes, textures, props,
  stickers, labels and motion feel, then apply them consistently to every scene, caption and graphic.
  Use the style only: do not copy its brand name, logo, handle, phone number, QR code or third-party
  app/brand logos. Replace those with generic icons or my own product name.
- If no design system is attached: use a clean, premium editorial look (one strong accent colour, bold
  condensed type for captions, an elegant serif for asides, mono for labels, subtle grain).

STEP 1: UNDERSTAND THE VIDEO FIRST
- Transcribe it word by word with exact timings. Tell me what was said, the structure (hook, problem,
  solution, proof, CTA), and every stumble, false start, repeated line, filler word and long pause.
- Check the framing, background, lighting and anything behind me that could break a cut-out.

STEP 2: CLEAN EDIT
- Cut every stumble, false start, retake, filler ("umm", "uh") and dead pause. Keep it natural, with
  no clipped words, and keep a tiny breath between phrases.
- Keep my best delivery of each line, and keep the strongest line as the hook in the first 2 seconds.

STEP 3: FULL EDIT (cue everything to the exact spoken word)
- Kinetic captions on every word, placed BEHIND me using a person cut-out (matte), with key words
  highlighted in the accent colours. Captions must stay readable and never cover my face.
- A different visual treatment for each section of the script (background changes, scene changes,
  transitions), so the reel never looks static. Vary it, but stay inside the design system.
- Motion graphics that illustrate what I'm saying at the moment I say it (icons, counters, checklists,
  UI cards, before/after, format frames, etc.).
- Camera work: punch-in zooms, slow pushes, impact shakes, beat pumps, smooth transitions/whip pans.
- Colour grade my footage so it looks clean and premium; match it to the design system.
- A clear end card with my CTA.

STEP 4: SOUND
- Clean and level my voice (de-noise, EQ, compression).
- Original, copyright-free background music that fits the mood, ducked under my voice, with a
  build/drop timed to a key moment in the script.
- Sound effects on every cut, transition, caption slam and graphic (whooshes, pops, clicks, hits,
  risers, typing), mixed so the voice always stays on top.
- Final loudness -14 LUFS.

STEP 5: COMPARISON REEL
- Raw take and final edit side by side, in sync with the final audio, labelled RAW and EDITED,
  styled in the same design system, with live counters (pauses cut, number of cuts).

SPECS
- 1080x1920, 9:16, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, under ~30 MB each.
- Keep all text and key graphics inside the Instagram Reels safe zone (clear of the top bar, the
  right-hand buttons and the bottom caption area).

QUALITY CHECK BEFORE DELIVERY
- Show me still frames from every section first, fix anything that overlaps, gets cut off or covers
  my face, then render. Check the full video once more and tell me honestly about anything that
  could still look rough.

My product / brand name: [YOUR PRODUCT NAME]
My CTA: [e.g. "Tap Learn More" / "Comment VIBE" / "Link in bio"]
Anything to keep or avoid: [optional notes]
```
